WHAT IS THIS?

A free match game for football fans. Pick a Nations League match, take penalties, then answer a few multiple choice questions.

Who wins? How many goals? Who scores? Once the match starts, a live board ranks everyone. Every match has its own board, and it disappears 24 hours after the match.

Example: Sam answers 6 questions for Portugal v Wales. At 12' Ronaldo scores, so Sam's "Ronaldo scores" answer turns green and his % jumps. Later Wales equalise, his "Wales score 0" answer turns red and his row slides down.
